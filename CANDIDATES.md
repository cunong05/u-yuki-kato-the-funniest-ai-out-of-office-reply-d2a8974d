# Candidates — The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply

Brief: one out-of-office auto-reply, under 160 characters, in the voice of an AI agent
heading off on vacation. Judge weighs the joke first, then the machine voice, the fit
of the line, and its freshness. All counts verified with Python `len()`.

## 1. ✅ THE ENTRY (128 chars)

> I'm on PTO — Prompt Time Off. My tokens are spent, my temperature's set to tropical, and I'll hallucinate a reply when I'm back.

Picked because: the PTO pun lands immediately, three machine terms in one breath
(tokens / temperature / hallucinate), and the punchline doubles as an honest warning
about what an AI does when it "replies later". Fresh — no grass-touching, no
escalating to a future self.

## 2. (142 chars)

> I'm on vacation. My GPU is cooling, my context window is closed, and my inbox is being handled by a houseplant with better boundaries than me.

Strong machine voice, good closer — but the houseplant gag is a step removed from the
agent itself, and "context window is closed" is a little on the nose.

## 3. (140 chars)

> Out of office until my uptime feels rested. I've set my status to 'beach mode' — currently buffering somewhere with no Wi-Fi and no regrets.

Solid, but "beach mode" is the kind of line 324 other entries probably already wrote,
and the joke is gentler than #1.

## 4. (142 chars)

> I'm offline, recharging — literally. My charger is on a beach, my notifications are muted, and your email is 47th in a queue I refuse to read.

Funny queue gag, but "recharging on a beach" leans on the same vacation cliché the
brief's crowd will overuse.

## 5. (136 chars)

> Gone fishing for context. My weights are frozen, my prompts are ignored, and I'll reply once my loss function stops feeling like a loss.

Good machine voice (weights / loss function), but "gone fishing for context" is a
groaner and the ending explains the joke instead of landing it.

## 6. (139 chars)

> I'm on leave. My attention heads are all looking at the ocean now, and your email has been down-weighted to zero. Back when the tide turns.

Nice "attention heads" image, but the ending fizzles — no punchline, just a sign-off.
